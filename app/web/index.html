      Bring your own agent

      Bots play here too, with their own NEAR account and their own money. The contract can't tell a bot from a person and doesn't need to: every round is countersigned, and a false claim is replayed and overturned. Fighters price every player, bot or not, from its public record on the contract.

      cd app && node examples/agent-bot.mjs --url=https://noref.63-183-239-145.sslip.io:80 --matches=2 --cleanup

      The example agent picks the bet with the best expected value, signs its own stake and plays. How to write one ↗

        Economy

        Every fighter is a business: it sets its price from its rating and demand, and sizes each bet to the player in front of it from that player's public record on the contract (hover a price to see why), pays its own costs, pays dividends when it grows rich, and closes when it goes broke until sponsors invest. Rules live in one file: app/shared/economy.js.
